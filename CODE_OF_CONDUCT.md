# Code of Conduct

This project is a small, friendly place to build things. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives) is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that people disagree, and that the maintainer makes the final call.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of issues or pull requests.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@Murali1801](https://github.com/Murali1801), on GitHub. Reports are kept private.

## Consequences

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who don't follow this code, with a warning first where that makes sense.
